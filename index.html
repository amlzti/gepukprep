    Where the data comes from Sumber data

    • Day of week Real calendar
    • Public holidays Real Perak 2026 holiday list. The report sets no holiday multiplier, so the owner adds a note on a holiday.
    • UTP status Real UTP September 2026 calendar, as in the report: lectures end 27 Nov, study break 28 Nov - 2 Dec, exams 3 - 15 Dec, semester break from 16 Dec
    • Starting values Report Section 3.4: normal weekday 117 portions, weekend ×1.24, rain ×0.85, study break ×0.95, exam week ×0.75, semester break ×0.40
    • Recipe Report Table 2: 1 chicken piece, 225 g rice (201-250 g), 28 g sambal (25-30 g) per plate
    • Daily sales Simulated the report's 30-day table (21 Nov - 20 Dec 2026), rainy days as chosen in the report. Semester-break rows use ×0.40 as stated in the report.
    • Weather for the day you plan Real Open-Meteo forecast for Seri Iskandar (up to 16 days ahead, plus the last 7 days). 10 mm or more = rainy. Dates with no forecast yet are left as dry for the owner to change.
    • Days saved in Tutup kedai Real entered by the crew
